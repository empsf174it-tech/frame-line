About Frameline

Cutting through the hype to bring you objective, rigorous camera reviews.

Frameline was born out of frustration. The internet is flooded with spec-sheet reading and hype-driven reviews that don't reflect what a camera actually feels like on set or in the field.

We are a team of working photographers, cinematographers, and colorists who test gear in real-world scenarios. We care about rolling shutter, dynamic range usability, skin tones, and reliability when the temperature drops.

How We Test

Our rigorous methodology ensures consistent and comparable scoring.

Dynamic Range & ISO

We use standardized charts back-to-back to measure usable stops of dynamic range, not just the marketing numbers. ISO variance is tested with controlled lighting setups.

Autofocus Tracking

Cameras are put through extreme sports scenarios and low-light portraits to push the subject detection algorithms to the limit.

Thermal Limits

For cinema and hybrid cameras, we run continuous recording tests at max resolution until thermal shutdown occurs in a controlled 72°F environment.

The 10-Point Scale

Our editorial score is a strict 1-10 scale. A 5 is average. A 9 or 10 indicates class-leading performance that pushes the industry forward.

Editorial Independence & Affiliate Disclosure

Frameline maintains strict editorial independence. We do not accept payment for reviews, and manufacturers have no say in our scores.
